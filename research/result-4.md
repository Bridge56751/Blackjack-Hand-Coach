## Key Facts

### Late surrender (American hole-card/peek; total-dependent)
Wizard of Odds defines late surrender as available only *after* dealer checks blackjack. Its total-dependent table has the following exact cells (all unlisted hard totals/upcards = do **not** surrender):

| Decks | S17: surrender | H17: surrender |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | hard 16 vs 10 | hard 15 vs A; hard 16 vs 10,A; hard 17 vs A |
| 2 | hard 15 vs 10; hard 16 vs 10,A | hard 15 vs 10,A; hard 16 vs 10,A; hard 17 vs A |
| 4, 6, 8 | hard 15 vs 10; hard 16 vs 9,10,A | hard 15 vs 10,A; hard 16 vs 9,10,A; hard 17 vs A |

Important table interpretation: its “16” means 10+6 or 9+7; **8,8 is a separate pair decision**, not automatically hard-16 surrender. Exact pair exception from Wizard’s composition table: surrender 8,8 vs A in **2D H17 only if no DAS**; surrender 8,8 vs A in **4D/6D H17**; 8D total- and composition-dependent surrender coincide. Wizard’s 4–8D text confirms H17 modification “surrender 15, a pair of 8s, and 17 vs A.”

**Fallback when surrender is unavailable:** preserve the ordinary hit/stand result, never substitute stand merely because surrender was preferred: hard 15 vs 10/A → H; hard 16 vs 9/10/A → H; hard 17 vs A → S. For the single-deck-only 16 vs 10 and H17-specific 15/16/17 cells, the same fallback rule applies. Pair 8,8 fallback is normally P; in particular Wizard gives splitting 8,8 vs 10 EV better than surrender even without DAS.

URLs: https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/surrender/ ; https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/strategy/4-decks ; https://wizardofodds.com/ask-the-wizard/blackjack/basic-strategy-hands

### Double restrictions: machine rule and explicit fallbacks
Use a two-stage table/action resolver, rather than maintaining a separate hand-written no-double table:
1. Compute the exact **unrestricted** action for `{decks, S17/H17, DAS, surrender}`.
2. If it is `D` but initial two-card total is outside the selected double range, select the chart’s embedded fallback: `Dh => H`, `Ds => S`. (Wizard calculator explicitly defines `Dh` and `Ds` that way.)
3. Do **not** replace a pair’s split action with a double restriction; resolve P first. If split is unavailable due to split-limit, re-resolve as hard total (Wizard 4D guide).

For **9–11 only**, all hard 9/10/11 double cells remain eligible; every otherwise-recommended soft double is illegal. Exact normal-action fallbacks for those disallowed soft doubles: soft 13–17 → H; soft 18 vs 2–6 → S (vs 9/10/A it was H already; vs 7/8 S already); soft 19 vs 6 → S. The single-deck S17 calculator chart also has hard 8 double-if-possible vs 4–6; with 9–11 it becomes H. (This hard-8 feature must be retained in any exact 1D table; it is absent in the published 4–8D text chart.)

For **10–11 only**, additionally convert every hard-9 `D` to H, and retain the 9–11 soft/hard-8 conversions above. Thus a code-level fallback map is: `D[total<10] => H` except when the underlying cell is marked `Ds` (then S); ordinary hard-9 D cells always H. This handles soft totals consistently because soft doubles’ non-double choice is hit except soft 18/19 D-to-stand cells.

This is supported directly by Wizard’s calculator legend (`Dh=double else hit`, `Ds=double else stand`) and 4–8D textual strategy: hard 9 vs 3–6, hard 10 except 10/A, hard 11 except A; soft 13/14 vs 5/6, 15/16 vs 4–6, 17/18 vs 3–6. H17 differences include double 11 vs A, soft 18 vs 2, soft 19 vs 6. URLs: https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/strategy/calculator ; https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/strategy/4-decks

## Claims Requiring Cross-Reference
1. **DAS influence on all late-surrender total rows:** Wizard’s primary total-dependent LS table is parameterized only by decks/S17-H17, while it separately calls out 8,8-v-A DAS exceptions. Treat its hard-total rows as authoritative baseline, but validate every `(DAS, no-DAS)` pair cell in the app engine, especially 2D H17 8,8vA.
2. **Restricted-double changes to split EV:** banning doubles after a split can alter P/H choices; “double 9–11” may be intended to apply after split too. The supplied source UI labels DAS separately but exact rule semantics need an app product decision. Do not assume a simple D→H/S transform covers changed split decisions.
3. **Exact 1D and 2D restricted-double matrices:** published static sources establish the transformations and notable hard-8 single-deck cell, but do not print all 60 combos. Generate/query each concrete ruleset and snapshot-test it before claiming a complete app matrix.
4. **Late-surrender timing:** findings assume US peek/hole-card. Do not reuse under ENHC/no-hole-card, which BlackjackTrainer treats as a strategy-affecting rule.
