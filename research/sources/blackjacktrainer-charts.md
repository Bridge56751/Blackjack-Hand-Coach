### Two kinds of rule controls

Rules are grouped into two tabs. Affects strategy holds the inputs that change which cells are correct — decks, H17/S17, DAS, surrender, peek, and resplit. Affects edge only holds rules that move the house edge but not the chart, like payout and side rules. Match both tabs to your real table for an accurate edge.

FAQ

What is a blackjack basic strategy chart?It is a table that tells you the best play for a given hand against a given dealer upcard. The correct cell depends on the table rules, which is why this chart lets you set decks, H17/S17, DAS, surrender, and other conditions before you study.

Which rule sets and decks does this chart cover?All common variants are covered with full basic strategy and Hi-Lo deviations: single-deck, double-deck, four-, six-, and eight-deck shoe games, in both H17 and S17, plus DAS, surrender, ENHC, peek, and resplit options. The chart and the deviation tables update for every combination.

Does H17 change basic strategy?Yes. H17 means the dealer hits soft 17, which gives the house a slightly stronger rule than S17. It changes a small set of close decisions and raises the house edge, so the chart should match the exact table you are playing.

What is a true count deviation?A true count deviation is an index play where the count makes a different decision better than basic strategy. In Hi-Lo, the running count is adjusted for decks remaining; when the true count reaches an index, the chart can switch that cell to the deviation play.

What are the Illustrious 18 and the Fab 4?The Illustrious 18 are the 18 highest-value Hi-Lo playing deviations, popularized by Don Schlesinger. They are paired with the Fab 4 — the four highest-value surrender deviations — and with the insurance index at TC +3, which together cover the most profitable count-based decisions for a Hi-Lo counter. The full list, with H17 and S17 indexes side by side, is on the [blackjack deviation chart](https://www.blackjacktrainer.fyi/charts/deviations).

Can I share a configured blackjack chart?Yes. Share chart copies a link that captures your table rules, deviation roster, and true count preview so anyone can study the same setup instantly. One click of Apply to Play loads those settings into the trainer for a live practice session.
